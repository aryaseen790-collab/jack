About & FAQ

What this site is, where the prices come from and answers to common questions.

About this guide

Jack Menu Guide is an unofficial fan site that collects the Jack in the Box menu and typical prices in one easy-to-browse place. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Jack in the Box Inc. To order, use the official Jack in the Box website or app, or visit a restaurant.

How prices are researched

Jack in the Box doesn't publish one national price list, and most restaurants are franchised, so each sets its own prices. We compared several menu-price trackers (listed on the sources page) and show the most common price for each item. When sources disagreed, the card also shows the lowest and highest price we saw. Prices tend to be higher in big cities, airports and California.

FAQ

Is breakfast served all day?

At most locations, yes. Jack in the Box is known for all-day breakfast, but hours vary by restaurant.

When can I get a Munchie Meal?

Munchie Meals are a late-night menu, usually from 9 pm to 5 am. Some locations offer them at other times.

What's the cheapest item?

The classic 2 Tacos, usually about $1.79 to $1.99. The app sometimes offers them for less.

Are many locations open 24 hours?

Many are, especially the drive-thru. Check your local restaurant's hours in the official app or on its map listing.

Why is the price at my restaurant different?

Franchise owners set their own prices based on local costs. Delivery apps also usually charge more than ordering in the restaurant.

Where can I find nutrition and allergen information?

Jack in the Box publishes official nutrition and allergen information on its website and in its app. We don't list calories here because they change with recipes and sizes.

Something is wrong or missing. How do I tell you?

Open an issue on this site's GitHub repository with the item, the price you saw and your city.
