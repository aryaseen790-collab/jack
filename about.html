About & FAQ

Jack Menu Guide is an unofficial fan site that puts the Jack in the Box menu and typical prices in one place. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Jack in the Box Inc. To order, use the official Jack in the Box website or app, or visit a restaurant.

How prices are researched

Jack in the Box doesn't publish one national price list, and most restaurants are franchised, so each sets its own prices. We compared several menu-price trackers (listed on the sources page) and show the most common price for each item. When sources disagreed, the board also shows the lowest and highest price we saw.

Things to try

  • On the home page, drag the box's crank clockwise. Turn on the music box for sound.
  • Press ⌘ K (or Ctrl K, or /) on any page to search.
  • Set a budget on the home page and we'll build the best meals that fit.
  • Tap + on any item to add it to your receipt.

FAQ

Is breakfast served all day?

At most locations, yes. Hours vary by restaurant.

When can I get a Munchie Meal?

Munchie Meals are a late-night menu, usually from 9 pm to 5 am.

What's the cheapest item?

The classic 2 Tacos, usually about $1.79 to $1.99. The app sometimes offers them for less.

Why is the price at my restaurant different?

Franchise owners set their own prices based on local costs. Delivery apps also usually charge more than the restaurant.

Where can I find nutrition and allergen information?

Jack in the Box publishes official nutrition and allergen information on its website and in its app.

Something is wrong or missing. How do I tell you?

Open an issue on this site's GitHub repository with the item, the price you saw and your city.
